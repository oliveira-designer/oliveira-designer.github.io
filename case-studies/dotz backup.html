01

Project Overview

Dotz is one of Brazil's largest loyalty networks, operating a B2B2C model that connects millions of consumers with hundreds of retail partners through points-based incentive programs.

I led the design of a CRM campaign management platform built to let commercial executives sell and configure marketing actions for retail partners, and give those partners visibility into campaign performance and business health. The flagship initiative was the Buying Mission campaign: a high-frequency CRM mechanic that drives consumer foot traffic and purchase frequency at Dotz's retail locations.

Impact

$120K/mo in operational savings
+10% revenue growth
−20% campaign cycle time

02

Constraints I Led

No shared product direction

The platform already existed, built entirely from the development squad's assumptions, with no discovery process behind it. Before any design work could happen, I had to establish a shared understanding of what the product actually needed to be, and for whom.

Top-down delivery pressure with no PO or PM buffer

For three months, the team had no Product Owner or Product Manager. I absorbed that gap running discovery, prioritization decisions, and aligning with stakeholders while continuing to lead design. When the commercial team and board drove scope, I created a lightweight process that could absorb those pressures without abandoning product integrity.

No direct access to primary users

The commercial department blocked all direct contact with retail partners, citing past friction with external consultants. I worked within that constraint redirecting research toward internal commercial consultants, who turned out to be the actual daily users of the platform a finding that reoriented the entire product direction.

03

Business Objectives

The platform needed to increase contract volume with retail partners, accelerate cross-selling of CRM actions, and eliminate the manual overhead dragging down campaign execution.

Every design decision was evaluated against those commercial outcomes, not just usability or process improvement.

04

Design process

User journey mapping

Mapping the full campaign contracting journey across commercial, CRM, and retail touchpoints revealed where the process broke down and which user owned each failure point.

Personas

With no direct access to retail partners, I actively monitored persona validity throughout discovery, using internal proxies and triangulating against behavioral signals from commercial consultants.

Platform adherence

I tracked whether shipped features were actually reducing manual work for the primary user, not just satisfying stakeholder requests. This became the north star when top-down pressure pushed for scope expansion.

Phase 1: Discovery
05

Research plan

Initial goal

The campaign creation UI for system administrators aimed to simplify the process for Dotz's CRM and commercial teams. My goal was to provide a light, minimalist, and straightforward experience, allowing teams to register campaigns autonomously, without CRM team intervention.

Desk research

The first step was to immerse myself in the business: What is retail? Which areas are involved? What are CRM actions and what are they for? I used Notion and FigJam to document and organize the research, which surfaced two crucial questions: Are we developing in the right direction? Should we focus on external or internal users?

06

User interviews

This phase was essential for validating several hypotheses raised during the Discovery. In-depth interviews were conducted with internal users from Dotz (commercial, business, CRM, Analytics, and Data Engineering areas). We not only understood how to solve the initially identified problem, but also discovered new issues that had not yet been identified.

The final user has changed

The platform was originally conceived as a self-service system for retail partners. However, during the discovery process, I realized this objective changed. The retail partners would rarely use the platform, as they would always be assisted by Dotz's commercial consultants — who would be the ones using the system daily.

Information about the partner's business needed to be centralized

The commercial team expected the portal to centralize a partner's business information in one place. At the time, finalizing a campaign hire involved multiple departments, and a lot of information got lost, causing noise and communication breakdowns.

Autonomy for the partner — but only at a later stage

Dotz Consultants wanted retail partners to eventually operate the platform on their own. However, at the beginning we didn't have a realistic view of partner feedback, since the partners using it didn't have a specialized CRM and insights team.

07

CSD Matrix

I decided it would also be a good idea to create a CSD matrix to easily map everything I already knew and what I wanted to uncover — considering both campaign creation and the retail segment as a whole.

"The platform was built for retail partners — but the real daily user was Dotz's own commercial consultant, invisible in every initial assumption."
— Discovery insight · User Interview phase
Phase 2: Definition
08

Personas

The earlier stages provided valuable inputs for developing personas involved in the campaign creation process. I categorized these personas into primary (daily platform users) and secondary (those who benefit from or are influenced by the platform's usage). Below is the primary persona that guided the rest of my process.

A

Afonso Moreira

Dotz Consultant — Account Executive

Age
29
Location
Campinas — São Paulo
Education
Bachelor's in Business Management
Bio

Afonso, account executive at Dotz for over a year, supports the Savegnago supermarket in São Paulo's interior. Part of a small but efficient team, he manages CRM campaigns for regional retail partners.

His demanding role involves presenting results to top management and frequently creating spreadsheets to share campaign outcomes with suppliers, making the process manual and time-consuming. Afonso dreams of a centralized system to streamline campaign result analysis and simplify his work.

Goals
  • Support the relationship manager by identifying and recommending business opportunities in structured retail.
  • Analyze and develop business strategies tailored to each partner.
  • Coordinate with suppliers to create campaigns aligned with budgets from both partners and suppliers.
Needs & expectations
  • Gain a clear and concise understanding of the large retailer's business status.
  • Quickly assist the retailer in selecting relevant campaigns.
  • Enable campaign simulations by SKU, akin to current Excel methods.
  • Automate CRM campaigns and insights for partners.
  • Centralize all information for analyzing campaign results in one place.
Pain points & frustrations
  • Creating numerous spreadsheets for campaign results increases workload.
  • Campaign selection is slow, relying on analyses from other Dotz areas, delaying responses to partners.
  • Decentralized partner information affects response time, risks data loss, and hinders strategic decision-making.
  • SMS limits per partner (2/day, 10/week) in PMWEB restrict campaign negotiation and implementation.
Memorable quotes

  • "The drive for sales to the industry is directly linked to the partner's adoption of the platform."

  • "Visualizing SKU availability is very important to us because it helps us segment actions and sell to the industry. In the industry, we have to sell all the time."

  • "We're trying to make the industry pay directly to Dotz, but it's a bit complicated…"

  • "I hope that [the platform] will be a way to greatly improve our work dynamic, since today we need to collect information [about the partners] from various sources."

  • "Contact with the industry is still very manual."

  • "Sometimes, I take some very good actions that go unnoticed… But anything that is done wrong ends up getting a lot of visibility because of the consequences it brings."

09

Service blueprint

User journey map — "As is"

In addition to creating product personas, I conducted an in-depth mapping of the journey for offering and contracting campaigns. I used a Service Blueprint to represent the journey, as it effectively highlights the touchpoints between the platform, the business, and the supporting processes.

I used FigJam to create this tool, adapting it to align with the specific needs of our project. My goal was to make it highly visual for stakeholders, incorporating images of each persona at the points in the flow where they play a role.

10

Benchmark

To design an effective solution, I conducted a business and visual benchmark analysis to explore market approaches to CRM campaign management and identify gaps for differentiation. To achieve this, I reached out to and analyzed several companies in the field.

"I hope that [the platform] will be a way to greatly improve our work dynamic, since today we need to collect information from various sources."
— Afonso Moreira · Dotz Consultant, Account Executive
Phase 3: Execution
11

Usability testing

To validate the collected information, I conducted usability tests with users previously interviewed during the discovery phase. These tests utilized low-fidelity prototypes designed based on discovery findings.

I used Maze for this process, as it offers detailed reports with valuable insights. Additionally, I leveraged the opportunity to gather behavioral data from users.
