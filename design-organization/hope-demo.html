Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is the only typeface. Sizes come from the --fs-* tokens. Display, H1, H2 and body-xl are fluid and reach the Figma size at their maximum, so the pixel size shown is for the current window. In the Figma size/* variables Mobile equals Desktop for h3, body, body-sm, label and body-xs; the lead (body-xl) is Desktop 19px and Mobile 16px (code syntax var(--fs-body-xl)); display, h1 and h2 stay responsive. body-xl is the only fluid body-text token and its endpoints match Figma; it interpolates between about 941px and 1118px, so values such as 17.408px at 1024px are expected, not a divergence. H3, body, body-sm, body-xs and label-caps are fixed. Weight, line height and tracking follow the Figma Foundation text styles as written in DESIGN.md.
