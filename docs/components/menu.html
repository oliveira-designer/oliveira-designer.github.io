Menu

A panel of actions or options opened from a trigger: 36px items, optional groups separated by a divider, a danger tone for destructive actions and a check on the chosen option.

When to use

  • A short list of actions on an object, opened from a button.
  • The options of a Select (the same panel look).
  • Put destructive actions last, after a divider.

Accessibility

The trigger has aria-haspopup="menu", aria-expanded and aria-controls. Selected in Figma means a chosen option, so it is menuitemradio with aria-checked="true" and a visible check; it is not announced by color alone. Tabs, Pagination and Menu mark the current item in three different ways on purpose: Tabs with a bottom line and text color, Pagination with a dark fill, Menu with a light fill, SemiBold text and a check. The divider is a role="separator". Danger is carried by the text and its position after the divider, not by color alone. Danger has the same five states as Default (10 variants). Measured contrast, Light and Dark: Danger text on the panel, also in Focus, 5.87 and 6.88; Danger Selected text and check on the Background/Default/Secondary fill, 5.48 and 6.12; Danger Disabled is the same as Default Disabled and exempt.
