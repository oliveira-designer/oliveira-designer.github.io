Bottom Sheet

A modal panel anchored to the bottom edge of the screen, for narrow and touch screens: a grabber, a title, a short body and a required footer with the actions stacked at full width. It blocks the page until the person decides.

Live example

Publish changes?

The changes become visible to the whole team. You can undo the publication later.

Delete project?

The project and all its files will be deleted. This action cannot be undone.

When to use

  • A short decision on a narrow or touch screen: confirm, cancel, choose.
  • Keep it short and about a single decision.
  • A destructive confirmation uses the destructive Button as the main action, and the focus starts on Cancel.

When not to use

  • A wide screen: use the Modal.
  • Extra context tied to an element, or a small control: use a Popover, which does not block the page.
  • A list of actions on an object: use a Menu.
  • A long form or a flow with several steps: use a page.
  • A message the person must read and cannot dismiss (an error or a mandatory reading): not available yet, see the gap below.

Anatomy

  • Scrim: a full-screen layer behind the panel (--color-bg-scrim), the same as the Modal. A click on it does not close the sheet.
  • Panel: the dialog, as wide as the screen and anchored to its bottom edge, with a border on three sides, a radius of 16 on the top corners and Shadow/lg.
  • Grabber row: a 32 by 4 bar in Border/Utilities/Input. It is decorative (aria-hidden), not focusable and not a button.
  • Content: the title (Heading/H2) and the supporting text (Body/Regular/body-sm, Text/Default/Secondary). There is no close X.
  • Footer (required): the primary Button on top and the secondary Button below, both at full width.

States

Bottom Sheet states, what changes in each and the tokens involved
State What changes Tokens
Closed The dialog and the scrim have the hidden attribute. Nothing is rendered. none
Open The scrim covers the page, the sheet sits on the bottom edge, the page behind is inert and its scroll is locked. --color-bg-scrim --z-modal-scrim --z-modal
Dragging While a pointer drags the grabber row or the title, the panel follows it down (never above its resting place). It has no transition: it moves with the pointer and goes back at once. none
Short screen The panel is as tall as its content up to the screen minus 16px; the body scrolls, the grabber row, the title and the footer stay in view. --space-4
Home indicator The bottom padding adds env(safe-area-inset-bottom), which is 0 unless the page has viewport-fit=cover. --space-6

Keyboard and ARIA

Bottom Sheet keyboard interaction
Key Action
Tab and Shift+Tab Move through the controls of the sheet. The focus wraps from the last to the first and cannot leave the sheet.
Escape Closes the sheet and returns the focus to the trigger.
Enter and Space Activate the focused button (the primary action or Cancel).
Swipe down (pointer) Drag the grabber row or the title down: past 30% of the height, or a fast flick, it closes like Cancel. It is an enhancement: the sheet works without any drag.
  • The panel has role="dialog" and aria-modal="true".
  • aria-labelledby points at the title and aria-describedby at the body.
  • The grabber is decorative: aria-hidden="true", not focusable. The swipe down is not announced; Cancel is the way out that does not depend on a gesture.
  • The focus moves into the sheet on open (to the first control, or to the element with data-bottom-sheet-initial-focus) and returns to the trigger on close. In a destructive confirmation, put data-bottom-sheet-initial-focus on Cancel, because the primary action comes first in the DOM.
  • The page behind is inert and the scroll is locked while the sheet is open.
  • When the body scrolls it gets tabindex="0", so the keyboard can scroll it.
