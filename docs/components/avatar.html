Avatar

A circle with the initials of a person in three sizes (32, 40 and 56), or their photo.

Live example

RM RM RM LL Luiza Laus
HTML 
<span class="avatar avatar--small" role="img" aria-label="Renan Moura">RM</span>
<span class="avatar avatar--medium" role="img" aria-label="Renan Moura">RM</span>
<span class="avatar avatar--large" role="img" aria-label="Renan Moura">RM</span>
<span class="avatar avatar--medium"><img class="avatar__image" src="../../images/testmonial_avatars/sarah.jpg" alt="Sarah Camêlo"></span>
<span class="avatar avatar--medium" aria-hidden="true">LL</span> Luiza Laus

When to use

  • Identify a person next to their name or on their own.
  • Three sizes: Small (32), Medium (40) and Large (56).
  • For a photo, fill the circle with the image and do not render the initials (the Figma text).

When not to use

  • An icon or a logo: use an icon or an image.
  • A status or a count: use a Badge.

Anatomy

  • Circle: --radius-pill, fill Border/Default/Default.
  • Initials: Small Body/SemiBold/body-xs, Medium Body/SemiBold/body-sm, Large Heading/H3. They are content, not generated.
  • Photo: the image fills the circle (object-fit: cover) and the initials are not rendered.

States

Avatar states, what changes in each and the tokens involved
State What changes Tokens
Small 32 by 32, initials in Body/SemiBold/body-xs semibold. --space-8 --fs-body-xs
Medium 40 by 40, initials in Body/SemiBold/body-sm semibold. --space-10 --fs-body-sm
Large 56 by 56, initials in Heading/H3. --fs-h3 --font-display
Photo An image fills the circle and the initials are not rendered. --radius-pill

Keyboard and ARIA

Avatar keyboard interaction
Key Action
None An Avatar is not interactive. If it is clickable, wrap it in a link or a button that has its own name.
  • Initials with the name next to them: aria-hidden="true", the name is already read.
  • Initials without the name next to them: role="img" and aria-label with the name (one announcement of the name instead of the letters).
  • Photo with the name next to it: alt="" (decorative).
  • Photo without the name next to it: alt is the name of the person.

Classes and tokens

Class Used for Tokens

.avatar--small Small, 32 by 32. --space-8 --fs-body-xs
.avatar--medium Medium, 40 by 40. --space-10 --fs-body-sm
.avatar--large Large, 56 by 56, Heading/H3. --font-display --fs-h3 --fw-bold --lh-140
