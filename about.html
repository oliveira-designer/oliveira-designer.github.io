About me

12 years of craft,
context, & impact.

Senior Product Designer based in Brazil. I've spent over a decade turning ambiguous briefs into shipped interfaces — across fintech, energy, SaaS, and loyalty platforms.

Background

Where I come from

Origin

I started my career in systems analysis and software development — which means I've always understood both sides of the product. That technical foundation makes my design decisions sharper and my engineering conversations shorter.

Trajectory

Over 12 years I've moved from junior developer to senior product designer, working in agencies, startups, and large enterprises. I've shipped work for Shell / Raízen, New Relic, Bradesco, Dotz, and Belvo — across Brazil and the US.

Today

I work across the full product lifecycle: discovery, flows, UI design, and engineering handoff. I integrate AI tools into my daily workflow to move faster and deliver more value per sprint without sacrificing quality.

Skills

What I bring to a team

Product Strategy

I connect user needs to business outcomes. Every design decision is tied to a metric, a constraint, or a strategic goal — not just aesthetics.

UX Research

Interviews, usability tests, surveys, and competitive analysis. I've led end-to-end research for complex B2B domains including FinOps and loyalty platforms.

UI Design & Systems

High-fidelity design, component libraries, and design tokens. I've contributed to and consumed design systems at scale in Figma.

Technical Foundation

Systems analysis and front-end background means I can read code, spot implementation constraints early, and write specs that engineers don't have to translate.

Cross-functional Leadership

I've led design end-to-end on multi-persona products, aligning PMs, engineers, and stakeholders — from initial brief to shipped feature.

AI-Augmented Workflow

I integrate AI tools into research synthesis, ideation, and prototyping — not as a shortcut, but as a force multiplier that frees up headspace for harder design problems.
